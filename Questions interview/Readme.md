PMCU — Programmable Microcontroller Peripherals Series

I’m starting to share my bilingual French / English engineering reference series dedicated to STM32 and PIC32 microcontrollers.

The series combines peripheral theory, practical configuration, calculations, register-level concepts, debugging, hardware considerations, code examples, and engineering interview questions.

The complete documents are available on GitHub. I’ll gradually add the different PMCU volumes to the repository.

Volumes currently shared:
PMCU-01 — DMA
PMCU-02 — GPIO
PMCU-03 — Interrupts
PMCU-04 — Timers & Counters
PMCU-05 — PWM, Capture & Compare

More peripheral topics will follow.
